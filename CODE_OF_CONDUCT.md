# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Opal to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project.
Report problems privately to the maintainer through a
[private GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/nirwwan/opal-browser/security/advisories/new)
(choose it even for conduct reports; it is private) or by contacting the maintainer on GitHub.
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions, and may
block people who don't follow this code.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
